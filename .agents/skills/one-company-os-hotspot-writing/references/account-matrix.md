# Five-Account Matrix

| Account | Category | Required reader question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Social and livelihood | How does this affect an ordinary person? |
| 2 | Law and event truth | What is confirmed, disputed, and legally attributable? |
| 3 | Money and cost of living | How could this change household income or spending? |
| 4 | International impact | Through what mechanism could this affect people in China? |
| 5 | People and business stories | Why did this person or company arrive at this outcome? |

Use the engine's account result. Change it only when a human supplies documented historical performance that makes a secondary category the better fit.

All accounts may use the same verified intelligence and structured data, but they never share finished copy. One event produces one account assignment unless a human approves and records a distinct event-angle identity for another account.
