# Quality Gates

## Decision gate

- A score of at least 75 is `immediate`; 60 through 74 is `candidate`; below 60 is `discard`.
- Any high risk produces `risk_pause`, regardless of score, urgency, or competitor activity.
- `risk_pause` stops account assignment and writing. Human review is required before continuing.

## Fact gate

- Every key fact has a primary or secondary source.
- Unconfirmed claims are labeled and excluded from definitive wording.
- Inference is never promoted to fact.
- Quotes are exact, attributable, and never fabricated.

## Originality gate

- The article adds an explanation, consequence, or documented context.
- It does not reuse another article's paragraph order or distinctive wording.
- One event produces one finished article unless a human approves and records a distinct event-angle identity.
- Shared intelligence and data may cross accounts; finished copy never does.

## Human review gate

Human review is mandatory for minors, casualties, privacy, medical conclusions, legal conclusions, financial conclusions, final headline choice, and every publish decision.
